Engage — WhatsApp automation mockups

Twenty screens from the Stitch project Webinar WhatsApp Automation Engine, downloaded whole and then made walkable: sidebars navigate, tabs and filters actually filter, dropdowns are built from the sample data, exports build a real CSV, a row's menu pauses or duplicates that row, the composer sends, the canvas takes a new step, the broadcast wizard walks all four of its steps, modals close. Where a control could only ever be faked it says so rather than doing nothing. Every screen carries a switcher at its bottom right for the ones no sidebar item names.
