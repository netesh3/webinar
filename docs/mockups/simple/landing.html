Built around what happens after you go live

Most webinar tools stop when the session ends. That's where follow-up starts.

One link to join

People register on your page and join in the browser. Nothing to install, and they don't need an account.

Reminders on email and WhatsApp

Set when reminders go out. Registrants who opt in to WhatsApp get them there too.

Follow-up by who showed up

See who came and who missed it, send each group the right message, and read replies in one inbox.

How it works

Four steps, in the order you already think about a webinar.

1
Schedule
A title, a date and a length. Sensible defaults for the rest.
2
Invite
Share your registration link. Reminders go out on their own.
3
Go live
Present, share your screen, take questions and run polls. It's recorded.
4
Follow up
Send the replay to people who missed it and a next step to people who came — on WhatsApp.
