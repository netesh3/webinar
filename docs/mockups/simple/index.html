Today

  • Home: 6 tabs, 5 buttons per card

Proposed

  • Home: 5 tabs, 2 buttons per card (rest in ⋯), a just-ended webinar's follow-up surfaced
  • Create: title, date/time/length → "Create & get link"; defaults shown as chips
  • Webinar page: a 4-step bar + 3 tabs per stage
  • Follow-up in one place: the webinar's Follow up tab (Messages keeps replies across webinars)
  • WhatsApp in the top bar; templates edited there only
  • Dead toggles removed
