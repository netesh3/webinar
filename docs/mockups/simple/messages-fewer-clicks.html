What each control does

Everything that changes sending is a labelled control on the row. The row turns grey on hover.

1
Switch turns it on

The only on/off control. Its 40×32 hit area is bigger than the track.

Clear signifier · Fitts's law
2
“When” is a dropdown

It has a caret and shows the choice, so you don't need to remember the options. Replay's two options are a segmented control.

Recognition over recall
3
“Send by” is two checkboxes

Labelled boxes with a border. On Highly engaged, Email shows hover (darker border) and WhatsApp shows keyboard focus (blue ring). Both can be ticked.

Affordance · consistency
4
Wording & preview opens under the row

A labelled button with a chevron. It shows the wordings and both previews side by side.

Progressive disclosure
5
One button for the group

Turns on the follow-ups that can send, and says which one it skips and why.

Efficiency · visibility of status
6
It can't be on until it can send

The switch stays disabled, the reason is on the row, and the fix is a button.

Error prevention
2 sendingStatus. Read-only, no border.
Control. Bordered, with a box.
