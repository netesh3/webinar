WhatsApp, explained on the page. The top says what this is and whether it works; under the connected number, five numbers for the period you pick (7 days · 30 days · All) — sent, delivered, read, failed (with why) and what Meta bills you — add up (212 sent = 206 delivered + 6 failed), and the same card sits on each ended webinar for just that webinar; replies waiting are on the Open inbox button. "What goes out automatically" is split into Before the webinar (confirmation and reminder, for everyone who registers) and After the webinar (the replay for everyone, then one card per group based on how they took part), so you can see who gets which message and which groups get nothing yet — switch or edit each one right where it is. Nothing is sent while you're live. Tap any message's time to change when it goes — reminders set a default for new webinars (still changeable per webinar), the replay goes when you publish or automatically after it ends, and each group's follow-up can go 1 hour, 2 hours, next morning or a day later. "Add an automatic reply" and "Set up" open a small step-by-step panel only when you ask; both dialogs are shown below the page.
