Tab 1 · The webinar. Everything about the webinar itself — basics (title, summary, description, tag, type, cover), when, registration, the room, and the feedback survey (the same builder, restyled to match: switch, type and title; questions with a live preview; when it pops up) — with no message settings in it. Registration and the room are shown open, not folded behind “Change”: without the message settings the tab is short enough to read top to bottom. The step card at the end hands over to tab 2 and says what is already on there. The bar at the bottom (date, seats, “6 messages on”) and Schedule are the same on both tabs, so a host can schedule from either one; tab 2 is never a required step.
