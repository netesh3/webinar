## How It Works: Infrastructure Model

### Phase 1: Launch (€10/mo server)
- All customers fit on your shared server
- 50 Starter + 200 Professional = 250 concurrent max
- Your €10/mo covers everyone
- **Gross margin: 99%**

### Phase 2: Growth (€10/mo + Dedicated Egress)
- Business customer ($199/mo) needs 300 concurrent
- You provision €60/mo dedicated egress (auto-trigger)
- Revenue covers cost 3.3x over
- **Gross margin: 99%** (shared customers), **70%** (dedicated)

### Phase 3: Scale (€10 + Multiple Dedicated Servers)
- 10 Business customers = €600/mo additional infrastructure
- 20 Enterprise customers = €2,200/mo additional infrastructure
- Total infrastructure: €2,810/mo
- Total revenue: €79,950/mo
- **Gross margin: 96%**

### Key Principle
**You never pay for infrastructure before you have customers paying for it.**

## Why This Works

✅ **Capital efficient**: €10/mo baseline, upgrade only when needed  
✅ **Profitable from day 1**: 99% margin at launch  
✅ **Scales with revenue**: Infrastructure costs grow slowly vs revenue growth  
✅ **No upfront investment**: Pay for capacity when customers need it  
✅ **Customer aligns with you**: When they upgrade tier, they help fund infrastructure

## TL;DR

**The Strategy:**
- Launch free + 3 paid tiers based on concurrent attendee capacity
- Don't pay for infrastructure upfront (€10/mo server covers launch)
- When customer needs more (300+ concurrent), you provision dedicated egress only then
- Customer's monthly fee (€199-499) covers infrastructure cost 3-5x over
- Result: 90-95% gross margins throughout

**The Result:**
- Month 6: $12.6K MRR (99% margin)
- Month 12: $80K MRR (99% margin)  
- Month 24: $267K MRR (98.7% margin)
- Profitable from Month 4 onwards
- $3M+ ARR potential by Year 2

**Why It Works:**
- Only pay for infrastructure when customers pay for it
- Most customers fit on €10/mo server (50-200 concurrent)
- Few customers need dedicated infrastructure (they pay premium tiers)
- Their premium tiers fund the infrastructure upgrade
- You never subsidize unused capacity
