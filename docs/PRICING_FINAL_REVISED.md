## Comparison: Trial Model vs Free Tier

| Aspect | Full Free Tier | Limited Free + 7-Day Trial |
|--------|----------------|---------------------------|
| Friction | Very low | Low (but CC required) |
| Abuse/Resource drain | High | Minimal |
| Conversion rate | 8-12% | 25-35% (trial) + 50% (paid from trial) |
| Support burden | High (many free users) | Low (committed customers) |
| Bandwidth usage | Significant | Minimal (free users stay small) |
| CAC for paid | High (pool is large) | Low (pre-filtered) |
| Churn rate | 20%+ | 8-10% |
| Revenue quality | Low (might cancel) | High (committed users) |

**Winner for bootstrapped startup:** Limited free + 7-day trial ✅

## Why Trial Model Beats Free Tier

### Problem with Full Free Tier
```
Free signup surge → 1,000+ free users in Month 3
  ↓
Watermark impressions help, but...
  ↓
Most never convert (why pay if free works?)
  ↓
Bandwidth bloat (10,000 free webinars × 100 attendees)
  ↓
Support burden (free users expect help)
  ↓
Infrastructure costs grow without revenue
  ↓
You're subsidizing non-customers
```

### Trial Model Benefits
```
Limited free → Starter users → Upgrade to trial
  ↓
45% try trial (pre-filtered)
  ↓
30% convert to paid (committed, CC on file)
  ↓
Auto-charge removes friction
  ↓
Only resource-heavy users are paying
  ↓
Infrastructure scales with revenue
  ↓
Profitable from Month 4
```
