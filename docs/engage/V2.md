# Engage v2: making the WhatsApp CRM feel great

Mocks: `docs/mockups/v2/index.html` (open in a browser; `render.sh` rebuilds the PNGs).
v2.1 mocks (after the Engagement tab landed): `docs/mockups/v2.1/`.
Built on v1 (`V1.md`); no new top-level menu, same WebinarLiv tokens.

## What the flow is today

1. **Before:** registration form tick box → confirmation → reminders at the webinar's times.
2. **During:** nothing on WhatsApp.
3. **After:** the webinar's Messages tab lists four follow-up buttons, reply names, automatic
   message counts and sent follow-ups. Attendees shows watch time and a "Message these N" bar.
4. **Replies:** Hosting → Messages, a two-pane inbox; the bell counts replies.
5. **Setup and automation:** `/host/crm` (Set up, Contacts, Broadcasts, Sequences, Bots).

It works, but everything has the same visual weight, so nothing tells the coach what to do next
or shows what WhatsApp achieved.

## What's wrong, by screen

| Screen | Today | Problem |
|---|---|---|
| Webinar → Messages | Five plain stacked lists | No story (before → live → after), no single next step, no results |
| Send dialog | Dropdown of raw names (`missed_you · en · marketing`), preview says "Thandi / Scaling Postgres" | Feels technical; preview isn't the real recipient; cost is unknown until the bill |
| Messages (inbox) | Blue bubbles, each automatic reminder as a full bubble, notes box under the composer | Doesn't look like WhatsApp; reminders bury the real conversation; no context about who this person is |
| People | Plain table with checkboxes | Can't see at a glance who is engaged or worth talking to |
| Hosting home / bell | Counts only | The payoff (read rate, show-up lift, leads) is invisible |
| Sequences / Bots | Empty pages explaining the concept | Blank-canvas problem: coaches don't build automations from scratch |
| Attendee's phone | Plain text templates | No buttons, images or calendar link; looks less polished than the host side |

## The plan

### Phase 1 — polish what exists (shipped)
- **Inbox thread looks like WhatsApp** (inbox mock): green outgoing bubbles, ✓✓ ticks, day
  markers that say what happened ("Joined live, watched 55 of 60 min"), automatic messages
  folded into one line, a pill showing time left in the 24h window.
- **Profile side panel** replaces the notes box under the composer: avatar, webinars with a
  watch bar each, tags, note, sequences.
- **Avatars everywhere** (list, People, bell), a hue per person as `ui.tsx` Avatar already does.
- **Send dialog**: template cards with friendly names and category pills; the preview uses the
  real first recipient with ‹ › to step through them, inside a phone frame; recipient summary as
  a 4-cell strip; the green "Send to 3 on WhatsApp" button.
- Fix the preview's hard-coded sample ("Thandi / Scaling Postgres").

### Phase 2 — tell the story and suggest the next step (shipped; reworked by v2.1)
- **Webinar → Messages becomes a journey** (webinar-messages mock): six steps from Registered to
  Replay with read rates, then four watch-time cards, each with a suggested message and "Review
  & send", then waiting replies and results side by side.
- **Next-step card** at the top: one rule-based action ("3 no-shows haven't heard from you").
  Rules only, no AI needed: no-shows with no follow-up, replies near the end of the 24h window,
  a published replay not sent yet.
- **Results**: show-up rate for WhatsApp-reminded people vs email-only, read %, replies, tagged
  leads, estimated Meta cost. All from data we already store (`notifications`, `crm_messages`,
  `attendance`).
- **"WhatsApp this week"** card on the Hosting home, plus a WhatsApp strip on each webinar card
  (next scheduled send, replies).
- **Bell**: one line per reply with a one-tap action.

## v2.1 — one place to decide, one place to see what WhatsApp did

The Engagement tab (#244–#247) is now what an ended webinar opens on. It overlaps the
Messages tab we built in Phase 2:

| Shown twice | Engagement tab | Messages tab (v2) | v2.1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attendance | Registered, attended, show-up, avg watch | "Live" journey step: joined, avg, show-up | Engagement only |
| Who to follow up | 5 groups by engagement score, "Schedule" (Coming soon, preview only) | 4 groups by watch time, real send | Engagement, by score, real send |
| Follow-up composer | `WhatsAppComposer`: fake templates, sends nothing | `SendDialog`: real templates, audience, cost | `SendDialog` only; delete the fake |
| "Follow up" button | Opens the fake composer | — | Scrolls to Follow up |

Two groupings of the same people (score tiers and watch-time buckets) is the worst part: a
coach sees "4 highly engaged" on one tab and "3 stayed to the end" on the next.

**Split:** Engagement = how it went and **who to message**. Messages = **what WhatsApp sent
and who answered**. Mocks: `v2.1/engagement.html`, `v2.1/webinar-messages.html`.

### Engagement tab (webinar code, Engage fills a slot)
- Follow up section: five cards (Highly engaged, Engaged, Passive, At risk, No-shows) with
  faces, the suggestion, and a state: Suggested → "Review & send", Scheduled → "Edit
  schedule", Sent → read/replied and a progress bar. Opens the real `SendDialog` with
  `audience: segment{ webinarId, tiers:[…] }` (no-shows: `attendance: not_joined`). The
  server already supports `tiers` (`crmstore.tierPredicate`).
- Everything above Follow up (overview, charts, attendees heatmap, chat, Q&A, polls,
  reactions, survey) stays exactly as it is. Only the Follow up section changes.
- Next-step card inside Follow up (moved from Messages): the first suggested group no one
  has messaged yet, using what the dashboard knows ("only 50% got the quiz right").
- The header "Follow up" button scrolls to the section instead of opening the fake composer.
- "Do this automatically after every webinar" toggle on the section (Phase 4 recipe).
- Remove `WhatsAppComposer`, the fake `TEMPLATES` and the "Coming soon" badge.
- Modularity: `components/engagement` renders `<EngagementFollowUp>`
  from `@/engage`; without WhatsApp configured it keeps today's read-only tier list.

### Messages tab (Engage)
- Remove: the "Live" step, watch-time cards, the next-step card, results that repeat attendance.
- Keep and sharpen: KPIs only WhatsApp knows (reach, sent, read, replies, estimated cost);
  one timeline of every message sent or queued for this webinar (confirmation, each
  reminder, each follow-up by group, the replay) with sent / read / replied and Cancel on
  scheduled ones; replies waiting; "Did the reminders help?".
- Nudge only for WhatsApp-specific things: a reply whose 24h window is closing.
- Before the webinar the timeline shows what will go out and when (same component).

### Attendees tab
- Chips switch from watch-time buckets to the same engagement groups; "Message these N"
  sends that segment. Watch time stays as a column.

### Backend (small)
- `GET /crm/webinars/{slug}/followups`: per tier + no-shows → count, reachable, faces, state
  (suggested / scheduled / sent with read and replied). Tier counts come from
  `engagement_scores`.
- `GET /crm/webinars/{slug}/timeline`: automatic sends grouped by kind and offset, follow-up
  broadcasts with segment labels, queued ones, replay step. Replaces the `journey` shape.
- `crm_broadcasts.segment` already stores tiers; derive the card state from it.
- Drop `watchBuckets` hints/suggestions from `engage/buckets.ts` once nothing uses them.

### Build order
1. Follow-up slot on Engagement with real send + remove the fake composer.
2. Slim the Messages tab to the timeline, WhatsApp KPIs and replies.
3. Attendees chips on engagement groups.
4. Then Phases 3–5 below as before (recipes now hang off the Follow up toggle).

### Phase 3 — speed for coaches who live in the inbox (about 1 week)
- **Quick replies**: saved snippets as chips above the composer (new `crm_snippets` table).
- **Keyboard**: ⌘↵ send, E done, J/K next and previous.
- **Snooze** a thread until a time (`inbox_snoozed_until` on `crm_contacts`).
- **Hot leads** filter in Messages and People.

### Phase 4 — automations people turn on (about 2 weeks)
- **Recipes** replace the empty Sequences and Bots pages (automations mock): Reminders, Replay
  for no-shows, Thank-you + offer, Keyword replies, Tag hot leads. Each is a toggle over the
  existing drip and bot engines with prefilled steps and a hint from the coach's own data
  ("would have reached 4 people"). The builders stay under "Build your own".
- **"Do this automatically after every webinar"** on Engagement → Follow up turns on the
  matching recipe per engagement group.
- Merge `/host/crm` into one **WhatsApp** page with three tabs: Automations, Templates, Number & billing.

### Phase 5 — the attendee's side (about 1 week, plus Meta approval time)
- **Opt-in** on the form lists what they'll get (confirmation, reminders, replay).
- **Richer templates**: header image (the webinar's cover), URL buttons (Join, Watch replay),
  quick-reply buttons (Can't make it, Tell me about the program). Needs template components in
  `wa/send.go` and the template sync (header, buttons) plus a "Create template" helper that
  submits our starter set to Meta.
- **Quick-reply taps become actions**: "Can't make it" tags them and queues the replay;
  "program" tags Hot lead.

### People (Phase 2–3)
- Summary strip (people, reachable, came back for a 2nd webinar, hot leads).
- Per row: avatar, a dot per webinar (attended, missed, upcoming), watch bar, 5-step engagement
  meter, last message with "needs reply".
- Saved groups ("Hot leads", "Came to 2+") and a floating bulk bar (Tag, Add to sequence,
  Message).

## Engagement score
Use the Engagement tab's score and tiers (`engagement_scores`); no separate Engage score.
People's meter shows the latest tier.

## Backend needed (small)
- `GET /crm/suggestions`: the rule-based next steps.
- `crm_snippets` table and CRUD; `inbox_snoozed_until`.
- Latest engagement tier in the People and Inbox queries.
- Template components (header, buttons) in sync and send; quick-reply payload handling in
  the webhook.
- Recipes: named presets that create a drip or bot from templates the host already has.

## Order I'd build it
1. v2.1 (above): follow-up on Engagement, slim Messages.
2. Phase 4 recipes (the Follow up toggle).
3. Phase 3 speed features.
4. Phase 5 rich templates (start Meta template submissions early).
